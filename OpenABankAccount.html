Open a Bank Account: What to Look For

A checking account in good standing is the foundation of your financial life — it is where your income lands, where your bill payments come from, and often the first relationship a bank uses to judge you for future credit products.

What to look for when choosing a free checking account:

  • No monthly maintenance fee, and no minimum-balance requirement to avoid one.

  • No overdraft fees — many online banks now decline a transaction for free instead of charging you $30+ for the privilege of being broke.

  • Free person-to-person transfers (such as Zelle®) so you can send and receive money without fees.

  • FDIC insurance (or NCUA insurance at a credit union) — never skip this.

Avoid any account that charges fees just to hold your own money. Every dollar you pay in bank fees is a dollar that cannot pay down your debt.

Curated account recommendations may be offered here in the future. For now, compare a few banks against the checklist above and pick the one with no fees, period.
